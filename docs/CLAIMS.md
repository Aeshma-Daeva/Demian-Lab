## Scientific Hierarchy

- **System:** Demian is a discrete-time nonlinear recurrent system,
  $z_{t+1}=F(z_t,x_t)$, with exposed readout $y_t=R(z_t)$.
- **Observation:** some exposed trajectories converge while measured hidden
  channels continue to change or separate.
- **Hypothesis:** projected fixed-point behavior can coexist with structured,
  continuation-relevant internal dynamics.
- **Control:** full-state versus surface-only continuation tests whether $y_t$
  is a sufficient continuation state; it does not establish a novel mechanism.
- **Interpretation:** surface attractor labels do not characterize the full
  state dynamics.
- **Untested speculation:** internal regimes align reproducibly with explicit
  channels and admit distinct Jacobian or finite-time Lyapunov signatures.

Operational AFP: $\Delta y_t \to 0$ with persistent structured change in
$z_t$, after excluding numerical drift, ordinary transients, and trivial
accumulation.
