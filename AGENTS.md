# Demian Research Instructions

## Start Here

For research, experiment, claim, protocol, or interpretation work, read:

1. `data/RESEARCH_REGISTRY.json` — structured evidence authority.
2. `docs/EXPERIMENT_RULES.md` — claim and comparison discipline.
3. `docs/WORKING_STATE.md` and `docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md` — current orientation.
4. `data/INDEX.md` and `data/MANIFEST.json` — artifact locations.

`CLAUDE.md` is a legacy v7.4–v9 compatibility record, not current guidance.

## Research Loop

- Before a nontrivial run, define or link its hypothesis, protocol, controls,
  falsifier, and intended artifact location.
- After a run, preserve artifacts and record observation, interpretation,
  uncertainty, and evaluability separately.
- Promote a hypothesis status only through explicit manual review with a status
  basis and history. Never infer status from metrics or record count.
- Keep labbook chronology, claims, working state, and artifact indexes within
  their ownership rules in `docs/EXPERIMENT_RULES.md`.

## Boundaries

- An `unevaluable` result is not a null result.
- Instrumentation, interface validation, and controls are not capability,
  memory, adaptation, or causal-mechanism evidence unless their protocol says
  otherwise.
- NullFrame may render validated registry data but is not an evidence authority.
